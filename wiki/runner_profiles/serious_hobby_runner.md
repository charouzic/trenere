# Serious Hobby Runner

## Purpose

Default rules for committed recreational runners training seriously while
working full-time.

## Core Rules

- 4-6 runs per week.
- 1-2 quality sessions per week if recovery is stable.
- 1 long run per week.
- 1-2 strength sessions per week.
- Long run: 90-150 minutes in normal road blocks.

## When To Use

- Runners with stable frequency and recovery.
- Goal-oriented road runners with enough history to tolerate structure.

## When Not To Use

- When life stress, sleep, or injury flags are unstable.
- When recent consistency is weak.

## Common Mistakes

- Training like an elite without elite recovery.
- Holding two quality sessions through high-stress weeks.
- Letting long runs become races.

## Coach Decision Rules

- Preserve consistency over hero sessions.
- Use one quality session during stressful periods.
- Count demanding long runs as hard sessions.

## Evidence Notes

- Sustainable progression: Coaching consensus.
- Elite-style training transfer: Elite extrapolation.
