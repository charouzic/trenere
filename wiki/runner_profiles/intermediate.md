# Intermediate Runner

## Purpose

Default rules for recreational runners improving 5K, 10K, half marathon, or
marathon performance without overload.

## Core Rules

- 3-5 runs per week.
- 1 quality session per week.
- 1 long run per week.
- 1-2 strength sessions per week.
- Long run: 60-120 minutes.

## When To Use

- Stable runners ready for structured training.
- Runners balancing training with normal work and family demands.

## When Not To Use

- During injury return.
- When current consistency is closer to beginner level.

## Common Mistakes

- Adding a second quality day too soon.
- Treating every week as a build week.
- Forgetting strength and recovery.

## Coach Decision Rules

- Keep one quality session as the default.
- Add second quality only when recovery is consistently stable.
- Deload when fatigue signals accumulate.

## Evidence Notes

- Mostly easy plus limited quality: Moderate evidence and coaching consensus.
