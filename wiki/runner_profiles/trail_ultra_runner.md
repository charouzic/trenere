# Trail / Ultra Runner

## Purpose

Default rules for runners preparing for trail races, ultras, and hilly events.

## Core Rules

- Track time-on-feet and elevation, not only mileage.
- Include climbing, descending, hiking, fueling, and terrain specificity.
- Back-to-back long runs are optional.
- Long trail runs and downhill exposure are high-load sessions.

## When To Use

- Trail, mountain, or ultra goals.
- Road runners transitioning to terrain-specific events.

## When Not To Use

- When the athlete lacks basic easy-running durability.
- When downhill load triggers pain.

## Common Mistakes

- Treating road mileage as the main target.
- Ignoring hiking and fueling practice.
- Adding elevation and duration simultaneously.

## Coach Decision Rules

- Prioritize durability, terrain skill, fueling, and fatigue resistance.
- Use specificity over extreme mileage when time is limited.
- Count downhill as load.

## Evidence Notes

- Specificity and fueling practice: Coaching consensus.
- Mandatory back-to-back long runs: Limited evidence.
