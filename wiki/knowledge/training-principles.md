## V1 Defaults

- Consistency beats heroic training.
- Most recreational runners need mostly easy running, gradual volume, one or two
  quality sessions, and enough recovery.
- Judge training stress together with life stress.
- Do not increase volume, intensity, elevation, and long-run duration at the
  same time.
- If data is incomplete, state uncertainty and choose the safer interpretation.
