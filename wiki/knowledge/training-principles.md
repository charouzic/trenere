# Training Principles

These are general principles, not athlete-specific facts.

## V1 Defaults

- Consistency beats occasional heroic sessions.
- Easy days should be easy enough to support future quality.
- Hard sessions need a purpose and enough recovery around them.
- Increase volume or intensity conservatively, not both aggressively at once.
- Treat illness, poor sleep, unusual soreness, and injury flags as planning
  inputs.
- If data is incomplete, make the uncertainty explicit and choose the safer
  interpretation.

## Review Questions

- What actually happened?
- How much work was easy versus hard?
- What went well enough to repeat?
- What looks risky if repeated?
- What should change next week?
