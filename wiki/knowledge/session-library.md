# Session Library

Reusable session patterns for planning. Adjust only after considering current
fitness, constraints, and injury flags.

## Easy Run

- Purpose: aerobic support and recovery-compatible volume.
- Guidance: conversational effort; avoid turning it into tempo.
- Fallback: shorten or walk-jog if fatigue or soreness is elevated.

## Long Run

- Purpose: aerobic durability.
- Guidance: mostly easy; avoid late surges unless planned and supported.
- Fallback: reduce duration by 20-40% if sleep, stress, or soreness is poor.

## Strides

- Purpose: neuromuscular speed without heavy metabolic cost.
- Guidance: relaxed, fast, full recovery, stop before form degrades.
- Fallback: skip if there is sharp pain, unusual tightness, or high fatigue.

## Threshold Session

- Purpose: controlled sustained aerobic power.
- Guidance: strong but controlled; should not feel like a race.
- Fallback: convert to steady easy running if fatigue or niggles are present.

## Recovery Session

- Purpose: circulation and rhythm with minimal stress.
- Guidance: keep it short and easy.
- Fallback: rest if the athlete feels worse after warming up.
