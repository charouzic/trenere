# Weekly Structure Examples

## Purpose

Simple defaults for planning before athlete-specific constraints are applied.

## Core Rules

- Respect available days.
- Do not cram missed workouts.
- Separate hard sessions when possible.
- Make easy days truly easy.

## Examples

- Beginner, 3 days: easy/run-walk, easy, long easy.
- Intermediate, 4 days: easy, quality, easy/strength, long easy.
- Intermediate, 5 days: easy, quality, easy, easy/strides, long easy.
- Serious hobby, 5-6 days: easy, quality, easy/strength, easy or second quality,
  easy/strides, long run.
- Trail/ultra: easy, hills or terrain quality, easy, strength, trail long run,
  optional short recovery.

## When To Use

- Weekly planning.
- Translating principles into a practical schedule.

## When Not To Use

- When injury, illness, or fatigue requires a recovery-only week.

## Common Mistakes

- Placing hard sessions back to back without reason.
- Hiding quality inside easy days.
- Keeping the same structure during high-stress weeks.

## Coach Decision Rules

- If recovery is poor, remove quality before removing all frequency.
- If time is limited, keep the most important session and simplify the rest.

## Evidence Notes

- Weekly structure examples are coaching consensus.
