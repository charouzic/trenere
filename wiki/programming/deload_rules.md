# Deload Rules

## Purpose

Reduce load before accumulated fatigue becomes injury, illness, or burnout.

## Core Rules

- Deload every three to five weeks or sooner if fatigue signs accumulate.
- Reduce intensity first during high-stress weeks.
- Reduce volume when fatigue remains high or soreness persists.
- Keep some frequency if easy running feels good.

## When To Use

- Poor sleep for multiple nights.
- Elevated resting HR or suppressed HRV versus baseline.
- Easy runs feel unusually hard.
- Unusual soreness, low motivation, or high life stress.

## When Not To Use

- As a substitute for medical evaluation when warning symptoms are present.

## Common Mistakes

- Deloading too late.
- Keeping the hard workout while cutting easy recovery runs.
- Cramming missed work after deload.

## Coach Decision Rules

- One bad signal: observe.
- Two bad signals: reduce.
- Three bad signals: replace hard work with easy/rest.

## Evidence Notes

- Recovery supports adaptation: Strong coaching consensus.
