# Return From Niggle Rules

## Purpose

Stage training back from pain without rushing intensity.

## Core Rules

- Pain 0-2/10 may be acceptable if stable and no gait change.
- Pain 3/10 requires caution and reduced load.
- Pain above 3/10 requires stopping or modifying.
- Any gait change means stop running.
- Worse next morning means the previous load was too high.

## Return Sequence

1. Walking.
2. Run-walk.
3. Short easy runs.
4. Normal easy volume.
5. Strides.
6. Hills or threshold.
7. VO2max or race pace.
8. Race-specific long sessions.

## When To Use

- After pain, flare-ups, or training interruptions.

## When Not To Use

- For sharp worsening pain, neurological symptoms, chest symptoms, fever, or
  systemic illness.

## Common Mistakes

- Reintroducing workouts before easy volume.
- Ignoring next-day response.
- Keeping speed while reducing only distance.

## Coach Decision Rules

- Reintroduce volume before intensity.
- Remove speed first if pain appears during fast running.
- Shorten duration first if pain appears late in runs.

## Evidence Notes

- Previous injury predicts future injury: Strong evidence.
