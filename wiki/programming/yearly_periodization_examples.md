# Yearly Periodization Examples

## Purpose

Offer simple annual structures without pretending recreational runners need
complex elite periodization.

## Core Rules

- Use reset, base, build, race-specific, taper, and recovery phases.
- Do not peak all year.
- Use maintenance during high life stress.
- Keep low-volume plans simple.

## Examples

- General fitness: base most of the year, short build blocks, regular deloads.
- Single goal race: reset, base, build, race-specific, taper, recovery.
- Two goal races: first race buildup, recovery, maintenance/base, second race
  build, taper, recovery.
- Trail/ultra: base durability, climbing/descending development, race-specific
  terrain/fueling, taper, recovery.

## When To Use

- Goal race planning.
- Deciding whether the current block should build, maintain, or recover.

## When Not To Use

- When immediate injury or illness management is needed.
- When the runner is not yet consistent.

## Common Mistakes

- Racing too often to build properly.
- Staying race-specific for months.
- Treating maintenance as failure.

## Coach Decision Rules

- If life stress rises, shift to maintenance.
- If base is unstable, delay specificity.

## Evidence Notes

- Simple periodization: Coaching consensus.
