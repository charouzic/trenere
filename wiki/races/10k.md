# 10K

## Purpose

Prepare for sustained hard running with enough aerobic support.

## Core Rules

- Prioritize easy volume and threshold development.
- Use VO2max sparingly.
- Keep long runs mostly easy.
- Practice pacing control.

## When To Use

- 10K goal race.
- Runners who need sustainable speed more than pure sprint speed.

## When Not To Use

- When recent training lacks consistency.
- During high stress or niggle return.

## Common Mistakes

- Turning threshold into race effort.
- Adding too much speed and not enough recovery.
- Neglecting long-run durability.

## Coach Decision Rules

- Use threshold intervals such as 3-6 x 5 min or 4-8 x 1 km.
- Add short faster work only after threshold and easy volume are stable.
- Reduce intensity first during poor sleep or high stress.

## Evidence Notes

- Threshold-style work is coaching consensus for 10K preparation.
