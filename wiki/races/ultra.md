# Ultra

## Purpose

Prepare for long-duration fatigue resistance, fueling, hiking, terrain, and
problem-solving.

## Core Rules

- Use time-on-feet and elevation as primary load markers.
- Practice fueling early and often.
- Include hiking when race-specific.
- Back-to-back long runs are optional, not mandatory.
- Downhill and technical terrain are high-load stimuli.

## When To Use

- Ultra-distance trail or road events.
- Long trail events where fueling and durability dominate.

## When Not To Use

- When basic durability is not established.
- During active injury return.

## Common Mistakes

- Chasing extreme mileage instead of specificity.
- Neglecting fueling and gut training.
- Making every weekend maximal.
- Assuming mandatory back-to-back long runs.

## Coach Decision Rules

- Prioritize durability, terrain skill, fueling, and fatigue resistance.
- Increase one major stressor at a time: duration, elevation, intensity, or
  terrain difficulty.
- Use back-to-backs only when the athlete absorbs normal long runs well.

## Evidence Notes

- Mandatory back-to-back long runs: Limited evidence.
- Fueling practice for long events: Coaching consensus.
