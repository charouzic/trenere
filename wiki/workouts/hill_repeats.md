# Hill Repeats

## Purpose

Build running strength, mechanics, and climbing ability.

## Core Rules

- Hills are intensity.
- Short hills can introduce speed with lower flat-ground impact.
- Downhill running adds eccentric load.
- Avoid with Achilles, calf, or knee flares.

## When To Use

- As a bridge from easy running to faster workouts.
- For trail and hilly race preparation.

## When Not To Use

- During calf, Achilles, or knee irritation.
- When downhill soreness would compromise the week.

## Common Mistakes

- Counting hill sessions as easy.
- Overdoing downhill exposure.
- Sprinting before mechanics are ready.

## Coach Decision Rules

- Options: 6-10 x 10 sec hill sprints, 6-10 x 60 sec uphill, or 4-8 x 3 min
  uphill.
- Keep recovery generous for short fast hills.

## Evidence Notes

- Hill use is coaching consensus; individual tolerance varies.
