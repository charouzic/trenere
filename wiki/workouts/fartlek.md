# Fartlek

## Purpose

Provide flexible speed variation when exact pace targets are unhelpful.

## Core Rules

- Control effort.
- Keep recoveries easy.
- Use as a bridge from easy running to workouts.
- Terrain-based surges are acceptable when purposeful.

## When To Use

- Early build phases.
- Variable terrain.
- Runners who overfocus on pace.

## When Not To Use

- During injury return.
- When the athlete turns every surge into a race.

## Common Mistakes

- Letting recoveries become moderate.
- Stacking fartlek with other hard sessions.
- Ignoring terrain load.

## Coach Decision Rules

- Options: 10 x 1 min hard / 1 min easy, 8 x 30 sec brisk / 90 sec easy, or
  terrain-based trail surges.
- Keep the workout repeatable.

## Evidence Notes

- Fartlek is coaching consensus.
