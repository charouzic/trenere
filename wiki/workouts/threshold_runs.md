# Threshold Runs

## Purpose

Improve sustainable hard running without racing workouts.

## Core Rules

- Controlled effort, not a race.
- RPE 6-7/10.
- Intervals are often safer than continuous tempo.
- Useful for 10K through marathon preparation.

## When To Use

- After easy volume is stable.
- When the runner can recover from one weekly quality session.

## When Not To Use

- During injury return.
- During high stress or poor sleep weeks.
- For beginners before consistency is established.

## Common Mistakes

- Running threshold too hard.
- Adding threshold on top of a demanding long run without recovery.

## Coach Decision Rules

- Prefer 3-6 x 5 min, 4-8 x 1 km, 2-4 x 10 min, or 20 min continuous tempo.
- Convert to easy running if fatigue or niggles are present.

## Evidence Notes

- Threshold work is useful but load-sensitive: Coaching consensus.
