# VO2max Intervals

## Purpose

Develop high-end aerobic power with a potent but costly stimulus.

## Core Rules

- Use short blocks.
- Usually no more than once per week.
- Stop if mechanics break down.
- Do not prescribe to beginners or runners returning from injury.

## When To Use

- After base and basic speed exposure are established.
- In controlled build phases.

## When Not To Use

- During niggle return.
- During high fatigue, poor sleep, or unstable recovery.
- As a default solution for limited training time.

## Common Mistakes

- Racing repetitions.
- Extending the session after form degrades.
- Pairing with another hard session too close.

## Coach Decision Rules

- Prefer simple formats: 6 x 2 min, 5 x 3 min, or 4-6 x 800 m with easy
  recoveries.
- Use strides, hills, or fartlek first if recent speed work is absent.

## Evidence Notes

- HIIT improves fitness but is costly: Moderate evidence.
