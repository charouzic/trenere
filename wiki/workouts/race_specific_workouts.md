# Race-Specific Workouts

## Purpose

Prepare the specific demands of the goal race.

## Core Rules

- Use only after base is stable.
- Increase specificity closer to race day.
- Do not race workouts.
- Include terrain, fueling, gear, and pacing.
- For trail/ultra, specificity often means terrain and logistics, not pace.

## When To Use

- Race-specific phases.
- When the athlete has enough durability for targeted stress.

## When Not To Use

- Too early in the block.
- During injury return or high life stress.
- When base consistency is still weak.

## Common Mistakes

- Using race pace too early.
- Practicing only fitness and ignoring fueling or terrain.
- Making every key session race-specific.

## Coach Decision Rules

- Match the race demand that matters most: pace, terrain, climbing, descending,
  fueling, gear, or duration.
- Keep enough recovery around specific sessions.

## Evidence Notes

- Specificity is coaching consensus.
