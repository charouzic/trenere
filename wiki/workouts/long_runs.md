# Long Runs

## Purpose

Develop endurance, durability, and fueling practice.

## Core Rules

- Usually once per week.
- Mostly easy.
- Cap by duration, not ego distance.
- Practice fueling after roughly 90 minutes.
- Avoid large jumps from recent longest run.
- Long runs are hard sessions even when easy.

## When To Use

- Base and race-specific endurance development.
- Trail/ultra terrain and logistics practice.

## When Not To Use

- During unstable injury return.
- When recovery signals are poor enough to require deload or rest.

## Common Mistakes

- Making the long run too large a share of weekly volume.
- Adding fast finishes before normal long runs are stable.
- Ignoring elevation and downhill as load.

## Coach Decision Rules

- If the week already has high stress, keep the long run easy or shorten it.
- If fueling will matter on race day, practice it before race-specific blocks.

## Evidence Notes

- Long-run durability is coaching consensus.
- Mandatory back-to-back long runs: Limited evidence.
