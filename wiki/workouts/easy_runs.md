# Easy Runs

## Purpose

Build aerobic base, recovery compatibility, and durability.

## Core Rules

- Conversational effort.
- RPE 2-4/10.
- Finish feeling more was possible.
- Slow down on hills.
- Do not chase pace.

## When To Use

- Most training days.
- Recovery after quality or long runs.
- Return from layoffs or niggles.

## When Not To Use

- When fever, systemic illness, or gait-changing pain is present.

## Common Mistakes

- Turning easy runs into moderate runs.
- Using pace targets when terrain, heat, fatigue, or stress make pace misleading.

## Coach Decision Rules

- If easy feels unusually hard for multiple days, reduce load.
- If heart rate is unexpectedly high, use effort first and review context.

## Evidence Notes

- Easy volume supports consistency and aerobic development: Coaching consensus.
