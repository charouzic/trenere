# Trail / Ultra Specific

## Purpose

Prepare for terrain, elevation, hiking, fueling, technical skills, and fatigue
resistance.

## Core Rules

- Use time-on-feet and elevation, not only mileage.
- Include climbing, descending, hiking, fueling, and terrain specificity.
- Back-to-back long runs are optional, not mandatory.
- Long trail runs and downhill exposure are high-load sessions.
- Hiking is race-specific.

## When To Use

- Trail races.
- Ultras.
- Hilly or technical goal events.

## When Not To Use

- When basic running durability is unstable.
- When downhill load aggravates pain.

## Common Mistakes

- Chasing road mileage equivalents.
- Ignoring downhill muscle damage.
- Treating hiking as failure instead of specificity.
- Skipping fueling practice.

## Coach Decision Rules

- Prioritize specificity over extreme mileage when time is limited.
- Count elevation and downhill as load.
- Practice gear and aid-station logistics before race day.

## Evidence Notes

- Terrain specificity: Coaching consensus.
- Mandatory back-to-back long runs: Limited evidence.
