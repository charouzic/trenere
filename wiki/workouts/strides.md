# Strides

## Purpose

Maintain speed and mechanics with low fatigue.

## Core Rules

- 4-8 x 15-25 sec.
- Fast but relaxed.
- Full recovery.
- Not sprinting.
- Stop before form tightens.

## When To Use

- After easy runs.
- Before adding interval training.
- During base phases to preserve leg speed.

## When Not To Use

- With sharp pain, unusual tightness, or high fatigue.
- During early injury return before easy running is stable.

## Common Mistakes

- Sprinting instead of running relaxed.
- Taking too little recovery.
- Adding too many repetitions.

## Coach Decision Rules

- Use strides before intervals when recent speed exposure is absent.
- Remove strides first if pain appears during fast running.

## Evidence Notes

- Low-fatigue speed maintenance: Coaching consensus.
